Voice Recognition & Privacy Notice
Strictly User-Initiated Microphone Access
DocuMind AI never listens in the background. Audio capture starts only when you click the microphone button and terminates immediately when you finish or cancel.
Zero Audio Storage
No audio is ever recorded, stored, or logged on DocuMind AI servers. All audio is processed ephemeral-only for transcription.
Browser-Based Web Speech API
Speech-to-text uses standard HTML5 Web Speech (SpeechRecognition). Audio is routed securely to produce accurate transcriptions.
Full Review Before Sending
Transcriptions are placed into the editable chat box first. You can always review, edit, or clear your question before submitting it to the AI.
